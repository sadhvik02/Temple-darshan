Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Sri Kedareshwara Ashramam mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you register for an account, book a Seva or Darshan, or make a donation. This includes your Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and Devotee/Gotra details.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to:

  • Process and manage your temple bookings and donations.
  • Communicate with you regarding your bookings.
  • Improve the app experience.

3. Payment Processing

We do not store your credit card, banking, or UPI details on our servers. All payments are securely processed by our verified third-party payment gateway (Razorpay).

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your data is kept strictly confidential and is used solely for temple administrative purposes.

5. Data Security

All data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard protocols. Access to your personal data is restricted to authorized temple administrators only.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the temple administration.
