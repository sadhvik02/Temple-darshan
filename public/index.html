❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I sign in to the Palgutta Ashramam mobile app?
Devotees can quickly sign in using their registered Mobile Number with a secure one-time OTP verification, or continue with basic details.
How can I book a Darshan or Seva slot?
In the app, navigate to the Ashrama Seva or Darshan tab, choose your preferred date and time slot, enter devotee details, and confirm your booking. Your digital pass will appear under Bookings.
Are online donations secure?
Yes. All donations for temple Annadanam and seva activities are processed securely through RBI-compliant, 256-bit encrypted payment gateways (UPI, Cards, NetBanking).
How can I delete my devotee account and data?
You can request immediate account and data deletion directly from the app's Devotee Profile tab, or visit our Account Deletion Request Page.
