Account & Data Deletion Request

Sri Kedareshwara Ashramam Mobile Application

How to request deletion of your account and associated data:

If you wish to permanently delete your account and all associated personal data from our systems, please send an email request to our support team.

Steps to Request Deletion

  1. Send an email from your registered email address to support@temple-ashramam.org (or contact temple administration).
  2. Include the subject line: Account Deletion Request - Sri Kedareshwara Ashramam.
  3. State your registered Full Name and Phone Number in the body of the email.

What Data is Deleted?

  • Your user profile, name, email address, and phone number.
  • Your saved preferences and gotra details.
  • Your authentication credentials in Firebase Auth.

Data Retention

Financial transaction records (such as completed Razorpay payment receipts and tax donation receipts) may be retained as legally required for accounting and auditing purposes, but will no longer be linked to an active user profile.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests are processed within 7 business days of verification.
